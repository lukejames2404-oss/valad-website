Valad to Attend the Real-World Asset Summit in New York

Valad will be attending the Real-World Asset Summit in New York on 1-2 September, bringing its perspective on the convergence of institutional real estate and on-chain finance to one of the leading events focused on the tokenisation of real-world assets.

  • Real-World Asset Summit, New York, 1-2 September.
  • Founder and CEO Eann Smith joining panel discussions.
  • Focus on real estate-backed credit and structured products on-chain.
  • Beyond fractional ownership to institutional-grade instruments.

What Valad will be discussing

Valad Founder and CEO Eann Smith will join the event’s panel discussions, sharing his views on how real estate can move on-chain and the role structured finance can play in bringing institutional property markets into the digital financial ecosystem.

The discussion will explore the evolution of real-world asset tokenisation beyond simple fractional ownership, including how real estate-backed credit, structured products and income-producing assets can be brought onto blockchain infrastructure.

The opportunities in on-chain real estate

Eann will also discuss the opportunities created by on-chain real estate, from greater transparency and liquidity to the potential for assets to interact with collateral markets, secondary markets and the wider DeFi ecosystem.

For Valad, the opportunity is not simply to tokenise buildings. It is to develop institutional-grade real estate financial instruments that can operate within emerging on-chain capital markets, while retaining the underwriting, collateral and structural protections expected from traditional finance.

About the summit

The summit brings together investors, asset managers, financial institutions, technology providers and leading participants across the digital asset and real-world asset ecosystem.

Valad’s attendance reflects its growing focus on the convergence of real estate and digital capital markets, and the firm looks forward to contributing to the next chapter of real estate on-chain.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the Real-World Asset Summit?

The summit takes place in New York on 1-2 September.

Who is representing Valad?

Founder and CEO Eann Smith, who will join the event’s panel discussions.

What will Valad be speaking about?

How real estate can move on-chain, and the role of structured finance in bringing real estate-backed credit and income-producing assets onto blockchain infrastructure.
